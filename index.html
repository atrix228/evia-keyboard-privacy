Privacy Policy

Evia AI Keyboard  ·  com.aikeyboard_evia

Effective date: October 27, 2026

About the App

Evia AI Keyboard is an AI-powered Android keyboard that provides features including AI chat, auto-translation across 34 languages, Chinese handwriting recognition, voice transcription, smart autocorrect, Telegram agent integration, a clipboard library, a built-in notepad, and customizable RGB themes. All AI features use API connections to either the Evia API or your own OpenAI-compatible API endpoint configured by you.

Information We Do Not Collect

Evia AI Keyboard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Specifically, the app:

  • Does not log or transmit your keystrokes or the text you type.
  • Does not collect personal information (name, email, phone number, device identifiers).
  • Does not use advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or crash-reporting services.
  • Does not access your contacts, location, camera, or files unless explicitly granted by you for a specific feature.
  • Does not require any account or sign-up.

Keyboard Permissions Disclosure

Android displays a standard warning when you enable any third-party keyboard stating it "may be able to collect all the text you type." This is a standard system notice for all IME apps.

Evia AI Keyboard does NOT collect or transmit your keystrokes. All text processing happens locally on your device except when you explicitly invoke an AI feature (chat, translate, autocorrect, voice).

AI Features and Network Access

Certain AI features require network access to function. When you actively use these features, the text you submit is sent to the AI service you have configured:

  • AI Chat (✨): Your messages are sent to the Evia API (apirouter.myevia.io) or your own custom API endpoint.
  • Auto-Translate (譯): The sentence being translated is sent to the configured AI model endpoint.
  • AI Autocorrect: Text is sent to the configured AI endpoint for correction. This feature is off by default.
  • Voice Transcription (🎤): Audio is sent to the configured transcription service.
  • Telegram Agents: Your messages are sent to the Telegram bot you configure — subject to that bot's privacy policy.

Your API key is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences and is never transmitted to us. It is sent only to the API endpoint you configure, as part of standard API authentication headers.

Please review the privacy policy of your chosen AI provider (e.g., Evia Lab, OpenAI, Anthropic, or others) to understand how they handle data you send to their APIs.

Handwriting Recognition

Chinese handwriting recognition uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition, which runs entirely on-device after the initial model download. No handwriting data is sent to external servers during recognition.

Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Your settings and preferences (keyboard size, theme, language, etc.)
  • Your API key(s) for AI services
  • Clipboard history (if you use the clipboard library feature)
  • Notepad content
  • Custom words added to your user dictionary

All of this data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can export and import your settings as a JSON file from within the app.

Third-Party Services

  • Google ML Kit — for on-device Chinese handwriting recognition. Subject to Google ML Kit Terms of Service.
  • Your chosen AI API provider — text sent only when you explicitly trigger an AI feature.
  • Telegram — only if you configure and use the Telegram Agents feature.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect data from any user, regardless of age.
