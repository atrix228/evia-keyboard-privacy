Privacy Policy

Evia AI Keyboard  ·  com.aikeyboard_evia

Effective date: October 27, 2026

About the App

Evia AI Keyboard is an AI-powered custom keyboard for Android featuring AI chat, auto-translation across 34 languages, Chinese handwriting recognition, voice transcription, Telegram agent integration, RGB effects, smart autocorrect, and n-gram next-word prediction.

When you use AI features (chat, translation, voice transcription, autocorrect), text is sent to the AI provider whose API key you configure. Your API key and all settings are stored locally on your device only.

Information We Collect

Evia AI Keyboard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. The app has no backend server of its own.

Specifically, the app:

  • Does not log, record, or upload your keystrokes or typed text.
  • Does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • Does not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, or cookies.
  • Does not access your contacts, location, camera, or files beyond what is required for the keyboard to function.
  • Does not require account registration.

AI Feature Data

Evia AI Keyboard optionally connects to third-party AI providers (e.g. Evia API, OpenAI-compatible endpoints) using an API key you supply. When you use AI chat, translation, voice transcription, or autocorrect:

  • The text you submit is sent directly from your device to the AI provider's servers over HTTPS.
  • Your API key is stored locally on your device and never sent to us.
  • The AI provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle your data. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the provider whose key you use.

You can use the keyboard in offline mode (basic typing, autocorrect, handwriting recognition after initial model download) without enabling any AI features.

Keyboard Permission Warning

Android displays a standard system warning when you enable any third-party keyboard stating it "may be able to collect all the text you type."

This is a standard Android warning shown for ALL third-party keyboards. Evia AI Keyboard does NOT collect or transmit your keystrokes. All text processing happens locally on your device unless you explicitly invoke an AI feature.

Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and local files:

  • Keyboard settings (size, theme, RGB settings, language preferences)
  • Your AI provider API key(s)
  • Clipboard library contents
  • Notepad contents
  • User dictionary words you add
  • Telegram agent configurations

All of this data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can export and import your settings as JSON from within the app.

Handwriting Recognition

Chinese handwriting recognition uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition, which runs entirely on-device after the language model is downloaded. Handwritten characters are never sent to external servers.

Voice Transcription

When you use voice input, the audio is sent to the AI provider you have configured (using your API key) for transcription. The audio is not stored on our servers. Refer to your AI provider's privacy policy for how they handle audio data.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services. The only third-party network requests are the AI API calls you explicitly initiate, sent directly to the provider you configure.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. No user registration is required to use the app.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
